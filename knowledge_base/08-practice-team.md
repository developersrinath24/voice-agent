# The practice team

## GP partners

- **Dr Anita Rao** is the senior partner and clinical lead. She has a special
  interest in diabetes and long-term conditions.
- **Dr James Whitfield** is a GP partner with an interest in minor surgery and
  musculoskeletal medicine.

## Salaried GP

- **Dr Priya Menon** works Monday to Thursday and has an interest in women's
  health and contraception.

## Nursing team

- **Sister Helen Boateng** is the lead practice nurse. She runs asthma, COPD,
  diabetes and blood pressure reviews and does cervical screening.
- **Kelly Doherty** is a practice nurse who covers immunisations, wound care and
  travel vaccinations.
- **Tom Aldridge** is the healthcare assistant. He takes blood samples, does blood
  pressure checks, ECG recordings and new patient health checks.

## Other clinical staff

- **Ravi Shah** is the clinical pharmacist. He reviews medicines, carries out
  medication reviews and can prescribe within an agreed plan.
- **Grace Okafor** is the mental health practitioner, available Tuesday and
  Thursday afternoons.
- **Diane Price** is the social prescribing link worker, available Monday to
  Wednesday.

## Non-clinical team

- **Louise Hart** is the practice manager and handles complaints, feedback and
  staffing matters.
- The reception team answers the telephone, books appointments and handles
  prescription queries.

## Who to ask for

| Problem | Best person to see |
| --- | --- |
| New or worsening symptom | Any GP |
| Asthma, COPD, diabetes review | Practice nurse |
| Smear test | Practice nurse |
| Medicine review or side effects | Clinical pharmacist |
| Blood test | Healthcare assistant |
| Low mood or anxiety | Mental health practitioner or self-referral |
| Housing, benefits or isolation | Social prescribing link worker |
| Vaccination | Practice nurse |

## Training practice

The surgery is a training practice and sometimes has GP registrars and medical
students. Patients are always told in advance and can ask to see a different
clinician.
