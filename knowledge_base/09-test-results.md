# Test results and referrals

## Getting your results

Results are reviewed by a clinician before they are released. The practice
contacts patients directly by text message or telephone when a result needs
action, such as a change of medicine or a repeat test.

If a result is normal and no action is needed, patients are not contacted and do
not need to call. Patients who still want to know the result can call reception
after the turnaround time below has passed.

## How long results take

| Test | Typical turnaround |
| --- | --- |
| Blood tests analysed on site | 3 working days |
| Blood tests sent to the laboratory | 5 working days |
| Urine tests | 5 working days |
| Swabs | 5 working days |
| X-ray or scan | 2 weeks |
| Specialist clinic letter | Up to 6 weeks |

## Referrals

When a GP refers a patient to hospital or a specialist service, the referral is
sent electronically, usually within two working days. The patient receives a
letter or text when the appointment is booked by the hospital. The practice does
not control hospital waiting times and cannot speed up an appointment unless
there is a clinical reason.

If a patient has not heard about a referral after four weeks, they should contact
reception rather than the hospital.

## Fit notes

Fit notes for statutory sick pay can be requested through the online service. The
first seven days of sickness can be self-certified without a fit note. Requests
are usually processed within two working days. Fit notes cannot be backdated
beyond the period the GP considers justified.

## Letters and reports

Letters for employers, insurers or schools are private work and are charged
according to the fee list.

## Access to your records

Registered patients can see their test results, medicines and immunisations in
the NHS App. A written copy of records can be requested from reception. The
practice responds to record requests within one month.
