# Appointments

## How to book

Patients can book an appointment in four ways:

1. **Online** through the NHS App or the online service linked from the practice
   website. This is available 24 hours a day.
2. **By telephone** on 01234 567 890 during opening hours. Option 1 is for
   urgent problems, option 2 for routine bookings.
3. **In person** at reception during opening hours, including Saturday mornings.
4. **Online consultation (eConsult)** for non-urgent problems. Submit the form any
   time and the practice responds within two working days.

Routine appointments can be booked up to four weeks in advance.

## Same-day and urgent appointments

A limited number of same-day appointments is released at 08:00 each weekday
morning. Requests for a same-day appointment are triaged by a clinician, usually
by telephone, before any face-to-face slot is confirmed. Patients should not
attend without an appointment, because the practice cannot guarantee being able
to see them.

## Appointment length

A standard appointment is 10 minutes and covers one problem. If a patient has
two or more separate problems, they should ask for a double appointment of 20
minutes so each issue gets proper attention.

## Types of appointment

Appointments are offered face-to-face, by telephone, or by video. The clinician
or the triage team decides which format is appropriate. Video appointments use a
link sent by text message and do not require any app to be installed.

## Cancelling and missed appointments

Appointments should be cancelled as soon as they are no longer needed, either
online or by telephone. Cancelling early allows the slot to be offered to another
patient.

Patients who miss three appointments in any 12-month period receive a letter
from the practice manager. After a fourth missed appointment, the practice may
require future appointments to be booked in person and confirmed 24 hours ahead.

## Home visits

Home visits are available for patients who are housebound or too unwell to
travel. Requests should be made before 10:30 where possible so they can be
scheduled that day. Home visits are not a way to avoid waiting for a routine
appointment.

## Waiting times

The surgery aims to answer 90 percent of telephone calls within five minutes.
Online consultation forms are answered within two working days. Routine
appointments are normally available within five working days, and same-day
appointments are always triaged on the day.
