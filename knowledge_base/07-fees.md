# Fees for non-NHS services

## Why there are charges

Some services are not covered by the NHS contract, such as private letters,
insurance forms and some travel vaccinations. These are charged privately. NHS
treatment and appointments for registered patients are always free.

## Current fees

| Service | Fee |
| --- | --- |
| Private sick note or letter confirming illness | 30 pounds |
| Letter confirming fitness to travel | 30 pounds |
| Passport or driving licence signature | 25 pounds |
| Insurance claim form or medical report | 95 pounds |
| HGV or taxi driver medical | 120 pounds |
| Travel appointment including risk assessment | 45 pounds |
| Travel vaccinations (per dose, varies by vaccine) | from 40 pounds |

Fees are reviewed each April. The fee is confirmed by reception before the
service is carried out.

## Payment

Payment is by card only, at reception. Payment is due when the document is
collected, not when it is requested. Documents are held for 30 days and then
destroyed.

## Turnaround

Simple letters are ready within five working days. Insurance reports and HGV
medicals can take up to 15 working days because they need a GP appointment and
time for the GP to write the report.

## What is still free

- All appointments about NHS treatment
- Repeat prescriptions
- Vaccinations on the routine NHS schedule
- Letters requested by another NHS service as part of ongoing care
- Fit notes for statutory sick pay, for the first seven days of absence and
  through the NHS fit note service

## Refunds

Fees are not refunded if a form cannot be completed because the GP decides it
would be misleading, but the patient is told before any charge is made.
