# Services offered

## Core GP services

Bramble Court Surgery provides general medical services for registered patients,
including consultations about new symptoms, ongoing conditions, mental health,
and preventative care. All routine NHS appointments are free of charge.

## Long-term condition reviews

Patients with asthma, COPD, diabetes, high blood pressure, kidney disease or
heart disease are invited for an annual review. Reviews are usually carried out
by the practice nurses, with a GP review where needed.

## Screening and vaccinations

- **Cervical screening (smear tests)** for patients aged 25 to 64, at the interval
  advised by the NHS screening programme. Appointments are with a practice nurse.
- **Childhood immunisations** following the routine NHS schedule, given by the
  practice nurses. Parents receive an invitation by text message.
- **Seasonal flu vaccination** clinics run from early October to the end of
  November, with walk-in sessions on Saturday mornings.
- **Shingles and pneumococcal vaccination** for eligible age groups.
- **NHS Health Check** for patients aged 40 to 74 who have not had one in the last
  five years. These take 20 minutes and include blood pressure, cholesterol and
  a lifestyle review.

## Contraception and sexual health

The practice offers contraceptive pill reviews, implant insertion and removal,
and coil fitting and removal. Emergency contraception is available. These
appointments are with a GP or a trained practice nurse and are longer than a
standard 10-minute slot.

## Minor surgery and procedures

Joint injections, cryotherapy for warts and skin tags, and removal of minor skin
lesions are performed in the treatment room. A GP referral is required before
booking minor surgery.

## Travel health

Travel advice and travel vaccinations are available. The practice does not offer
yellow fever vaccination and will refer patients to a specialist travel clinic
for that. Travel appointments must be booked at least six weeks before travel.

## Mental health and wellbeing

The practice works with a social prescribing link worker who can help with
housing, benefits, isolation and community activities. For talking therapies,
patients can refer themselves directly to the local NHS talking therapies service
without seeing a GP first.

## What the practice does not provide

The practice does not provide A&E or emergency care. Patients with an emergency
should call 999.

The practice does not X-ray or scan patients on site. X-rays and scans are
arranged at Kingsmere General Hospital.

The practice does not offer ear syringing. Patients who need ear syringing are
referred to a community clinic.

The practice does not provide yellow fever vaccination. Patients are referred to
a specialist travel clinic instead.

The practice does not provide dentistry. Patients with toothache should contact a
dentist, or call NHS 111 if they do not have one.

The practice does not provide optometry. Patients should contact an optician for
eye tests.

The practice does not provide physiotherapy on site, but patients can refer
themselves directly to NHS physiotherapy without seeing a GP first.
