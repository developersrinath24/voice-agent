# About Bramble Court Surgery

Bramble Court Surgery is a fictional NHS GP practice used as the knowledge base
for a demo voice assistant. Everything on this page is invented.

## Contact details

- **Address:** 17 Bramble Court, Kingsmere, KM4 2TR
- **Reception telephone:** 01234 567 890
- **Email:** reception@bramblecourtsurgery.nhs.example
- **Website:** www.bramblecourtsurgery.example

## Opening hours

The surgery closes at 18:30 from Monday to Friday. On Tuesday and Thursday it
closes later, at 20:00, during extended hours. On Saturday it closes at 12:00,
and it is closed all day on Sundays and on English bank holidays.

Reception and the telephone lines are open from 08:00 each weekday. On Saturday
mornings the front door is open for pre-booked appointments only; reception and
the telephone lines are not staffed.

## Getting here

There are 14 parking spaces including 2 disabled bays. Parking is free for up to
two hours and there is no barrier or ticket machine. The building is step-free
throughout, with an accessible toilet on the ground floor, a hearing loop at
reception, and wide doorways in all consulting rooms. Assistance dogs are
welcome. Bus routes 12 and 14 stop on Kingsmere High Street, a four-minute walk
from the surgery.

## Size of the practice

The surgery cares for approximately 9,400 patients. It is a single-site practice
and does not operate a branch surgery.

## Interpreting and accessibility support

Telephone interpreting is available for any appointment and can be arranged in
advance for face-to-face appointments. British Sign Language interpreters can be
booked with at least five working days' notice. Large-print information and
easy-read versions of common leaflets are available from reception on request.
