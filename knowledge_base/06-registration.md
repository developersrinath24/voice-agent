# Registering with the practice

## Who can register

Anyone living within the practice area can register, including babies, children
and people who have recently moved to the UK. There is no upper age limit and
registration is free.

## Practice area

The practice accepts patients living in Kingsmere, Millbrook, Ashford Vale,
Bramble Hill and Northgate. Patients outside this area can still ask to register;
the practice will usually agree where there are exceptional circumstances, such
as an existing family relationship with the practice.

## How to register

1. Complete the registration form, available at reception or from the practice
   website.
2. Provide photo identification and one proof of address, such as a utility bill
   or tenancy agreement.
3. Provide the name and address of the previous GP, if there is one.

Patients without identification can still register. The practice will accept
alternative evidence, such as a letter from a support worker, a bank statement or
a hostel address.

Registration is normally processed within two working days. New patients are
offered a new patient health check with the healthcare assistant, which takes
about 20 minutes and covers blood pressure, weight, smoking status and any
medicines currently taken.

## Children

A parent or guardian registers a child under 16. Children aged 16 and over can
register themselves.

## Temporary patients

Patients staying in the area for less than three months, for example while
visiting family or working locally, can register as a temporary patient for up to
three months.

## Visitors from overseas

Some patients visiting from overseas may be charged for treatment. The practice
follows NHS charging rules and will explain any charge before treatment is given.

## Changing practice

Patients who move away or wish to change practice do not need to tell the current
practice. The new practice notifies NHS England, and records are transferred
electronically.
